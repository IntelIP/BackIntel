Frontend dependencies

recorded in frontend/package-lock.json. DependencyLicenses contains available
LICENSE, LICENCE, NOTICE, COPYING, and OFL texts from those installed packages.

Python interface
PythonDemo/requirements.txt pins Reflex 0.9.8. Its dependencies are installed
separately under their upstream terms. Python wheels and environments are not
redistributed in this archive.

Interpretation and prediction methods
The retained run used the hosted Jev provider through TypeSafe/OpenRouter, plus
local CatBoost 1.2.10 and TabICL 2.2.0. Exact local runtime requirements are in
SourceSnapshot/requirements.models.txt. The package contains recorded results
and source, not model checkpoints, API credentials, provider SDK installations,
or a new grant to use a hosted model. Model and hosted-provider use remains
subject to its original terms and task-scoped authorization.

Dataset and claims
The business reports, measurements, and future outcomes in this package are
synthetic demonstration data. Real customer or production datasets are absent.
Earlier failed-request charges and local compute cost remain unknown. Recorded
predictions do not establish customer accuracy or measured commercial value.
