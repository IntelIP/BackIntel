BackIntel: a support lead's reports become a reviewable decision packet

Business objective
A support lead repeatedly reads customer messages, checks queue measurements,
collects evidence, and prepares a follow-up decision. Corrections force them to
repeat that preparation. BackIntel performs the approved preparation and keeps
the evidence current. A person still decides what action to take.

Current demonstration
Use business-v4. Its customer reports, measurements, and future outcomes are
synthetic. The interpretation and prediction executions are real. This recording
walks through saved execution evidence; navigating the report makes no model calls.
The earlier business-v3 run used short messages and remains an engine rehearsal.

Verified result
- Both scenarios completed: Support and Equipment.
- Jev returned 54 actual responses: 27 per scenario. No fixture responses were used.
- All 90 scheduled jobs completed. No pending triggers remain.
- CatBoost, TabICLv2, and the baseline actually ran. Support compared facts alone
  with facts plus Jev, using 18 earlier training records and six later validation
  records. These six records support selection, not an independent accuracy claim.
- Support selected CatBoost with Jev: probability error 0.1086. Baseline: 0.2531;
  CatBoost facts: 0.1264; TabICLv2 facts: 0.1671; TabICLv2 with Jev: 0.1575.
  Lower error is better on this synthetic validation set.
- Three source updates refreshed the Support packet. Superseded predictions lost
  authority. The corrected Accounts report says the service works; its current
  estimate is about 26.4%. Access still reports a login failure; its estimate is
  about 78.7%. These experimental estimates are not business outcomes.
- A labelled synthetic Access review was saved in React and read in Reflex.
- Explicit replay reused all 90 jobs without changing the stored domain result
  or making new paid calls.
