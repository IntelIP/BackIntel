BACKINTEL DEMO POC — SCOPE AND EVIDENCE

Prepared October 7, 2026 for source publication and demo review.

What a reviewer can do now
Extract BackIntelDemoBusinessV4.zip, check its manifest, run RunDemo.py, inspect
saved findings and evidence, and record a decision in the extracted copy.
The package contains synthetic business data and historical model results.
Opening it makes no new model calls and does not start a production workflow.

Current source capabilities
The analysis workspace supports saved single-domain goals, source permissions,
known-file importers, prepared snapshots, bounded analyst tools, local predictor
comparisons, corrections, usage accounting, durable jobs, and recovery.
The capability simulation and older review interfaces remain available as
separately labeled development surfaces. OlistV2 data remains removed.

Historical consolidation evidence — source revision 8f3cb28
162 Python tests passed. Of 56 scenarios, 50 passed, zero failed, and six were
blocked: Credit prediction and five live analyst cases. Offline browser and
workflow checks used simulated analyst/model responses with real local services.
Real local predictors ran separately on Commerce, Support, Churn, and Maintenance;
metrics matched the baseline. Saved predictions survived model reload.
Domains were tested separately, not as one combined dataset. Support tickets are
synthetic; NASA engine trajectories are simulated; Telco is a static snapshot;

Limits
Missing Credit files/confirmation and live analyst credential/spending
reconciliation blocked those cases. Model weights and datasets are not bundled
with the source. Historical receipts and the recorded archive are not acceptance
of a later commit. Hosted checks validate only their stated offline scope.
No claim is made for production readiness, customer accuracy, measured savings,
complete operating cost, general connectors, autonomous preparation of unfamiliar
data, or arbitrary agent-created pipelines.
