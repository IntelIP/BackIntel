# NOAA Global Summary of the Day

Publisher: NOAA National Centers for Environmental Information, US Department of
Commerce. The assigned files are original 2023 CSVs for three US stations. Read
the included original `gsod_readme.txt` for variable definitions, missing-value
sentinels, precipitation attributes, units, and observation-period qualifications.

The original files have not been reinterpreted, imputed, resampled or converted
to a different calendar/timezone. Source quality and observation periods must be
considered before interpreting a field. Preserve NOAA/NCEI attribution and do
not imply endorsement. The README's non-US commercial redistribution restriction
is why this package is restricted to US stations.

This note contains source context only, not private questions, observed quality
counts or reference answers.
