## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
Original roadmap story: S31. Original priority: P1. Work type: research.

## Business outcome

As a product owner, I need measured user usefulness, so that release decisions follow observed results rather than activity or assumed savings.

## Scope

Select the pilot audience and workflow; agree success targets before collecting results; compare task completion, total human effort, material mistakes, and known operating costs with the existing approach.

## Owned surface

Authorized pilot design and result report. Outreach and customer messages require explicit authority; none are sent by this plan.

## Prerequisites

S-35 and an agreed pilot audience, comparison, and authority.

## Next staged delivery

Determine whether the completed workflow is useful in a real agreed pilot. Compare it with the user's existing way of doing the same work, including setup and supervision, so lower effort or cost is demonstrated rather than inferred from a successful run.

## Decisions before selection

Select the real pilot participant, permitted business question and data, baseline manual or existing-tool process, observation period, usefulness measures, and success thresholds. Historical retail replay can support preparation but cannot replace the agreed real-use pilot.

These refinements record future work. Hudson selects one implementation story at a time; no implementation, model spending, live scheduling, commit, publication, or deployment is authorized by this planning update.
<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:END -->

## Acceptance Criteria
<!-- AC:BEGIN -->
- [ ] #1 Present the agreed comparison with limitations and a next product decision. Count checking and correction work as human effort. Unmeasured costs and unsupported value claims remain explicit; a synthetic demonstration cannot substitute for user-value evidence.
- [ ] #2 The comparison records equivalent task scope, setup effort, hands-on work, elapsed completion time, answer quality, errors, remaining intervention, and known operating cost for BackIntel and the agreed alternative. Unknowns and differences in scope are explicit.
- [ ] #3 The pilot participant records whether the answer and operating experience support the intended decision, and the report states which work BackIntel replaced, which work still needs a person, and whether the agreed success thresholds were met. A useful negative result is retained without inventing savings.
